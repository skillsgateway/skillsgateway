# upstream-sync Specification

## Purpose

Per-marketplace sync modes in front of the unchanged quarantine pipeline: the
on-demand default, the scheduled oldest-first polling sweep, the HMAC-authenticated
inbound webhook trigger, the explicit upstream-outage resilience guarantee, and the
audit trail of every mode change and sync-triggered ingestion.
