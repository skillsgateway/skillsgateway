## Purpose

Continuous re-vetting of content that is already approved and served: the scheduled oldest-first
sweep and the on-demand passes that re-run the vetting chain over approved snapshots, the
retroactive quarantine that revokes and unpublishes a snapshot whose fresh effective outcome
objects to its content, the warn-only mode that records the same violation without retracting
anything, the rule that a run which could not conclude never retracts, the lifecycle events and
fetch-ledger blast-radius report that make a violation actionable, the ledger record of every
run and transition, and the portal surface a reviewer answers a revocation from.
