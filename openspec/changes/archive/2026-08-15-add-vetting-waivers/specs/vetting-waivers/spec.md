## Purpose

Scoped, expiring accepted-risk exceptions against vetting findings: the waiver model and how a
waiver's scope is matched to a finding, the effective vetting outcome derived from the recorded
run plus the waivers active at that instant, expiry and revocation as the mechanism that restores
the block, the portal surface a reviewer manages waivers from, and the ledger record of every
waiver's creation, use, revocation and expiry.
