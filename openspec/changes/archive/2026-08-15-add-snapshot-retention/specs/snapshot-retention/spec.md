## Purpose

Criteria-based retention for snapshots: policy evaluation over quarantine age, supersession
and last-served time, soft deletion with a restore window, the guard that keeps approved
(served) snapshots out of reach, scheduled hard-delete compaction that reclaims git storage,
the audit record of every retention action, and the portal delete/restore surface.
