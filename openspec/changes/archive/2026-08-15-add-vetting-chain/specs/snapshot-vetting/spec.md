## Purpose

The pluggable vetting chain: ordered connectors run at ingestion against the quarantined,
SHA-pinned snapshot, fail-closed aggregation of their verdicts, the two built-in synchronous
connectors, the approval gate with a recorded reviewer override, the portal surface that puts
the evidence in front of the reviewer, and the ledger record of every run, verdict and override.
