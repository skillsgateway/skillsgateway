# adoption-reporting Specification

## Purpose

Read-only adoption and staleness reporting derived from the append-only fetch
ledger — fetch counts per marketplace, snapshot SHA and identity over a time
window, the identities whose latest fetch is no longer the served tip — and
the portal page presenting both.
