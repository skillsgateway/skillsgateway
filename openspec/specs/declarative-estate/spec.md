# declarative-estate Specification

## Purpose

Full configuration-as-code for the estate: a declarative
`skills-gateway.estate.*` block reconciled additively and idempotently at
startup and on demand, through the same validated and audited paths as the
API, with operator-supplied secrets by reference and per-entry failure
isolation.
