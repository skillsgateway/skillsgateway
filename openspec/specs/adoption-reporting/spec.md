# adoption-reporting Specification

## Purpose
Who fetches what through the facade, read from the append-only fetch ledger: the windowed per-marketplace and per-snapshot adoption report, the staleness report of identities not on the served tip, the skill-level presence report over the served commit trees, and the portal page that presents them.
