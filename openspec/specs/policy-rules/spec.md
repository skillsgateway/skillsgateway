# policy-rules Specification

## Purpose

Organizational prohibitions as data: CEL deny rules managed through an
audited API and the declarative estate, enforced fail-closed at the moment
of approval, recorded on the append-only ledger when they decide, and
testable against real snapshots in a read-only playground before they are
enforced.
